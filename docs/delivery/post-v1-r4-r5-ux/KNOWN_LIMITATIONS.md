## Functional boundaries

- Collaboration membership and scoped review work are real; branch-specific manuscript readers/writers remain unavailable. Unsupported source modes fail closed rather than falling back to the local manuscript.
- Author context uses the actual request builder and final dispatch authority. Removal operates on a provable whole automatic-context bundle and approved STYLE/PLOT references. Arbitrary per-source decomposition is unavailable. Direct multi-variant requests are local-only and unavailable when a broker budget/ledger/price policy applies; group cloud/budget reservations are not implemented.
- U01 complete cross-project layout/reference restoration and U10 isolated first-launch sample orchestration remain concrete UX gaps; existing saved anchors, notes, safe shortcuts and searchable enabled tools do not imply those are done.
- Portable export restores a selected manuscript/media subset into a new project; the original backup remains the full-history/Canon backup. Fork/merge and offline exchange support selected local rich-document sources, with explicit review, original CAS and unknown-write reconciliation.
- Offline synchronization has a real two-process loopback test and manual outbox/inbox protocol. No cloud transport is deployed or automatically contacted. Real-time co-editing is not implemented.
- Declarative workflows execute the registered deterministic reviewed chain. Custom model nodes and executable plugins are unavailable; plugin policy remains DENY_ALL. Templates are untrusted data.
- Manual multilingual editions, reviewed comic layouts and interactive story exports have real deterministic workflows. Automatic translation, model-created art, voice quality, arbitrary engine execution and complete production typography are not verified.
- ASR/forced alignment, OCR/vector/vision research adapters and unsupported media families remain explicit adapter boundaries. Literal checks, measured metrics and manual simulation are not literary-quality or semantic-understanding claims.
