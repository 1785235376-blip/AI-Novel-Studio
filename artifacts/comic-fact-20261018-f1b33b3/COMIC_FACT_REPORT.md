## Independent Comic access

IMAGE-only users can open the actual Image Studio Comic entry through a narrowly scoped bootstrap, create/edit/reopen source-free independent blank/text/geometry pages and books using original Comic owners. Read/write/review permissions stay distinct. Generic Studio, Graph, Prompt, ORA imports, native author archives and generation remain under their existing NOVEL authority.

The fallback is confined to Comic and is resource-aware. Source-derived pages, characters, screenplay/shot anchors and assets cannot be exposed by treating IMAGE as a blanket NOVEL grant. Catalog/list/direct-ID/history/restore/copy/book/publication paths retain source checks and original actor/project/branch/incarnation/CAS controls. Original NOVEL users remain compatible. Bootstrap is a read and does not initialize an incarnation marker. No new store or workflow engine.

## Read-only fact downstream uses

From an explicitly confirmed Story fact-use, the user selects one formal shot asset and explicitly inspects its saved Graph-node and Timeline-clip uses. The original fact and usage owners are reused. Before/after fact rereads and exact asset ID/version/digest/kind joins guard the assembled path.

Both owner reads must succeed. Denial, malformed evidence, changed pins or retired context clears the entire result. Other asset snapshots are labeled separately. Limits remain 25 documents / 200 references per owner; unknown, empty, truncated and unavailable are distinct. No automatic regeneration, mutation or execution. This is a bounded cross-owner observation, not an atomic snapshot or semantic causation proof; export, cost and unrecorded/recursive dependencies remain unknown.
